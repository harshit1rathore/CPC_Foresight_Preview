    Enter a CPC classification. The dashboard maps it to its subclass and shows how that field and its neighbours are forecast to move over the next quarters: activity, disruption (CD index), which fields are taking over its role, where new links are forming, and the words and companies driving it.

    Filing activity

    Share of all US applications filed in a quarter that fall in this subclass (4-quarter sum). Shaded: quarters still filling in as applications publish. Band: 80% forecast interval.

    Disruption (CD index)

    Share of patents in the top 10% of their grant-year cohort by 5-year CD index. Shaded: 5-year citation windows still open, so values there are provisional.

    CD index against neighbours

    This subclass and its four closest neighbours, 4-quarter pooled.

    Technology neighbourhood

    Closest subclasses by co-classification, citations and text similarity. Click a node to open it. Dashed links are white spaces: not linked today, forecast to link.

    Threats

    Fields forecast to take over the role this subclass plays (substitution), or close neighbours forecast to grow faster and be more disruptive (overtaking).

    Opportunities

    Pull: fields forecast to build on this subclass more. Convergence: co-classification forecast to strengthen.

    White spaces

    Subclasses that read like this one (top quarter by text similarity) but are not linked to it today, ranked by the forecast probability of a new co-classification link.

    Recent technology

    Distinctive terms in titles over the last two years, and terms whose share rose most in the last four quarters.

    Who is active

    Notable disruptive patents

    Recently granted patents in this subclass with the highest CD index so far.

    What the model looked at

    Neighbours the graph attention weighted most for this subclass in the latest quarter.

    How reliable is this

    Rolling-origin backtest at this horizon. Skill = error reduction against "no change" and against boosted trees that see only the subclass's own history. Per-subclass forecasts blend the GNN with graph-augmented boosted trees; "Model" shows the mix.
